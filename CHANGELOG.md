## 3.25.0

*This version is in preparation; this section grows with every test version.*

- **Looking up places in the research from the map**: with a tree that has a research, the map's status bar offers *In the research* (AI) beside the map's own lookup. It opens the same preview and *Queue* as *Places*, for the places without a position the research is not working on yet, and nothing is queued before *Queue*. When the map's lookup does not find some places, the bar says so and offers looking them up in the research; a followed place without a position offers it too. With the research on another computer or on a phone, the button says where it is done.
- **Who decides hypotheses**: with a research that supports it, *Research for this tree* chooses *The user decides* or *The AI decides*. *The user decides* is written to the research at once; *The AI decides* is confirmed in the research's terminal, and switching does not change hypotheses already decided. In *What the research knows* a hypothesis the agent decided says *decided by the agent* with the day and its reason (a long reason unfolds), and a hypothesis offers *Decide in the research* or, once decided, *Change in the research*, where the research on this computer offers it.
- **Confirming the research's transcription**: with a research that supports it, the source viewer offers *Confirm transcription* at the end of the line *Transcription read by the research*. The confirmation is written to the research at once and the line then says the transcription was confirmed in the app, with the day; *Undo confirmation* takes it back with one click. While the research is not running the line says confirming is possible once it runs, and a transcription the research changed meanwhile is said under the line, to be confirmed once the new wording arrives. The app's GEDCOM export keeps who read a transcription.
- **Image only in the research**: the filter and the tag in *Sources* read *Image only in the research*, the line under the filter says the images are stored only in the research on this computer and show in the app once loaded, and a *?* opens *How Strom works* at its rule.
- **Life timeline**: the line of a former partner ends at a dated divorce or separation instead of running on to today. A widowhood and a divorce without a date stay as they were.
- **Following the research**: the hollow marker on the map is named *no person on the map yet* instead of *outside the map scope*, the follow legend stands above *Back* also before the map's background is agreed to, and a late answer of the research no longer brings back the working mark after the agent's work has ended.
- **Help in forms**: the *?* at *Other spellings of the name*, *Godparents & witnesses* and the story's text opens *How Strom works* at the rule of that field instead of a popup with a long text. *Read a document*, *Hypotheses* in *What the research knows*, the preview of looking up places not on the map in the research and the follow legend have a *?* that opens their rule too.
- **Read a document**: when the research agent's app is on the computer but its terminal program is not, the reason says so instead of the general *reading can’t start right now*.
- **Conflicts**: a value the research wrote with a leftover source label in front of it (such as *@s:*) is shown without the label.
- **Editing while following the research**: while the research is followed, the person menu shows *Edit* greyed with why (*Read-only while following the research.*) and *Stop following* right under it. Once following stops, the one-time question *How should changes go to the research?* comes right away instead of later.
- **Screen readers**: closed dialogs and menus are no longer read out, icon buttons (zoom, filters, back and forward, photo rotation and others) have a readable name, a bottom sheet takes no taps while it slides in, and with reduced motion menus, notices and the bottom sheet appear without animation. *How Strom works* has a new chapter *Using the app*: where things are in the app, what the controls are called and editing while following the research.
- **Checking a place's location**: a place marked *Check* in *Places* offers *Show on the map* beside *Keep* and *Change*. The small map shows the location as it stands among the tree's other places before anything is decided, and the warning of a location far from the family's places, or in another district than the records name, adds *Possibly another place of the same name.*
- **Finding a place on the map**: the results of *Find on the map* lie where the tree lives. The small map fits the results in the tree's country, and results in another country hundreds of km away come last, folded under *More far away (N)* and only hinted at by their direction on the map's edge (a click opens their row). A result's distance and *near the other places* are measured by the family's places of that place, as the check measures, and a short legend says what the grey, green and ochre letters mean. A far or other-district warning adds *Possibly another place of the same name.*, and the rest of a sentence of a record (such as *v Lučici v*) is no longer asked or shown under *Also searched as*.
- **Reading a document with other agents and models**: with a research that offers it, *Read a document* says who reads (the research's agent with its model and effort), and *Adjust reading* chooses the agent, model and effort of each reader, for example another AI agent as the second reader. The choice goes to the research only when it differs from its settings; an agent that cannot read now (limit reached, sign-in expired) is said with the reason and the reading does not start. The progress, the result, the research panel and the list of readings say who read, and where two readers differ each version carries its reader's mark. On a phone each choice is a full-width field.
- **Reading a source again from its original**: with a research that keeps a source's original, the source viewer offers *Read again…* (AI) under the transcription, and beside the image for a source of one excerpt. The research reads the original again in full quality and compares the new reading with the transcription: the result says whether it matches or how many places differ. A difference becomes a task to check in the research (*Open the task*), not a conflict; the transcription and its confirmation stay as they are. On a phone the viewer says that reading again works in the app on the computer with the research.

## 3.24.0

*Released 6 November 2026.*

- **How Strom works in the app**: the whole documentation of the app and the research, in English, Czech, German and Polish, is part of the single file and opens without a connection, from *Actions*, *More* on a phone and *About Strom*. Contents by part (A · R · S), search by a word or a rule number (*R4.3*), a *Glossary*, links between rules with *Back*, and *On the web ↗* for the same page on the website. A rule newer than the research connected to the tree says so.
- **Help right at the rule**: a quiet *?* in the headings of *Queue*, *Awaiting action* and the agent's work in the *Research overview* and its small panel, of *Places*, *Places in the tree*, the conflicts panel and a person's *Conflicts* opens *How Strom works* at the rule that part follows.
- **Towns of the same name on the map**: when several towns share a place's name (Lhota), finding the place takes the one nearest the tree's other places instead of the first match, still with one request per place. The place's check says how many towns of that name the map found. Locations placed by hand or kept are never moved, neither by a lookup nor by a merged file.
- **Translation of a transcript**: a source whose note carries the research's translation of the transcript (of a Latin or Cyrillic register entry, say) shows it under the transcript in the source view and on the person page, folded as *Translation of the transcript*. The note itself stays as it was.
- **Awaiting action**: a task that is the only one of its place gets its place's heading with the place's actions, as any group, instead of a bare row that read as part of the place above. The kind chips wrap onto a second line instead of being cut off at the panel's edge.
- **The research's What's new from About**: the research's *What's new* in *About Strom* opens the same dialog with the research's points, read over its bridge, and *All research changes ↗* leads on to the research's changelog on GitHub.
- **Following the research in the fan and on the map**: *Follow in* (in the research's small panel, the overview and the strip on a phone) switches following between *Family*, *Fan* and *Map*. Work on a marriage is followed at the couple: the husband comes into focus, else the wife. In the fan the person worked on stands in the session's colour and flashes once when the work moves on and when following arrives in the fan; a person outside the fan gets a label at the nearest ancestor, clear of the panels and buttons. The fan keeps the centre it had before following, and a new centre comes in with a short cross-fade. On the map a ring marks the place worked on (solid for the research's place record, dashed for a place of the person) and pulses once when following arrives on the map; moving the map pauses its panning until *Back to the agent*, and a place without a position offers *Find position*. With several sessions each has its number and colour, and a fading trail shows the people and places worked on before. *Settings → Following research* chooses the view, the fan's centre and the trail. Smaller fixes: the map's zoom buttons stand clear of the research's panel, a message on a phone stands above the map's status bar, the overview's line about the followed tree no longer leaves an empty strip, following the tree already open is no longer announced, and the help mark of *Awaiting action* carries the section's name.
- **Live loading without a blink**: a change from the research is drawn in one go: cards that stay keep their place, a new person grows out of their line, a person taken away shrinks into it, a merged person flows into the one that stays, and a changed card is ringed in the session's colour. A new person out of view gets a notch at the edge, the zoom and the focused person stay, and a screen reader hears what changed. *Motion when changes load* in *Settings → Following research* turns the motion off, as reduced motion in the system does.

## 3.23.0

*Released 5 November 2026.*

- **Map**: the map background loads within the tile usage policy of OpenStreetMap however the app is opened. A tile sends the page's address, as the policy asks, and where none can be sent (the app saved as a file, a browser add-on that removes it) the tiles are asked for with the app's identification, no longer showing the server's *Access blocked* pictures. When the server refuses the background even so, one note on the map says so and the places stay on it.
- **Images and answers from the app**: an item in *Awaiting action* that waits for images, a website or a check is done in the app. *Add images* takes files chosen or dropped onto the item, gives them the numbers the task asks for (by file name, in order or by hand, also the pages of a PDF), checks them before sending and shows the progress; for an image already saved it asks *Replace* or *Keep the saved one*, a file the same as an image the research already has is not added, with a sentence naming that image, and a file the research had under that number already says *Already saved*. *Answer* writes a reply to the task, kept as a draft until it is sent, and the task goes back to the queue. The item shows how its images stand (*1 of 2 images*), the book with its numbers and what has just arrived; items of one archive stand together under it with its website and what it still misses, the archive's name on one line and its actions under it where they do not fit beside. The small panel offers one main action per item with its own dialog, and a phone gives the answer while images are added on a computer. This works with research 1.27.0; an older research takes the answer in its terminal.
- **The agent's browser**: with a research that says the state of its agent's browser, *The research is ready* and *Research for this tree* show a *Browser* row under the agent: *Claude in Chrome connected*, the extension missing (with a link to it) or an agent whose browser is not watched, and the archives still without permission, each with *Permission…* that opens it in the research. An item in *Awaiting action* waiting for images from an archive only the browser reaches says what would let the agent save them itself, once in the heading for two or more items of one archive. Both follow every status of the research; a phone shows them without links. An older research shows neither.
- **What's new from About**: *What's new* in *About Strom* opens the same dialog as after an update, over it, and its *All changes ↗* leads on to the whole changelog on GitHub.

## 3.22.0

*Released 4 November 2026.*

- **Research directions**: the *Directions* panel of the research overview manages the directions without the terminal. *+ New direction* takes a person, a kind (*Ancestors*, *Descendants* or *Question*), an optional question, a name and limits of generations and years; *Edit* changes them, and a direction that already exists is offered with *Show it* or *Create anyway*. Creating a direction costs nothing; the agent starts on it in the next research run.
- **Order of work**: the order of the directions is the order in which the research works on them, changed by dragging, with the arrows or *Move to the top*. *Pause* and *Restart* come with *Undo*, *End…* asks first and takes an optional reason; ended directions are folded at the end. Each direction shows what is being done now, its limits and what it found in the last days.
- **Directions of a person**: *Find ancestors* and *Find descendants* in the person menu open the form for that person, and a person in a direction has an *In direction* row in the *Research* menu and in the person detail.
- **Work outside the directions**: the panel lists the tasks outside the directions by branches, each with its nearest person and direction, a few examples and how it is taken: *Now and then*, *At the end*, *As usual* or *Do not research*, with *Undo*. A branch can be added to a direction or start a new one. A click on a branch unfolds its first tasks right under it, numbered by their place in the queue, with *All N tasks in the queue* for the rest. Each task of the queue says why it stands where it stands, and *Do next* or *To the end* moves it. A growing branch asks once in *Awaiting action* what to do with it; when the direction near it is paused, the branch is offered to the nearest running one. With research 1.26.0 the app reads the queue from the research page by page, never all of it at once: a branch unfolds its own tasks from the whole queue with their places, even far past the first tasks; the filters of a direction and of a branch count and show the whole queue; *Load more* adds 50 tasks at a time, up to 300 in the list, the rest through a filter. An older research says that a branch's tasks stand further on in the queue instead of an empty list. A task about nobody says its way outside the directions in the same words (*now and then*, *at the end*, *never*).
- **Research for this tree**: *Work outside the directions* sets how these tasks are taken by default (*Now and then*: about one per ten tasks of the directions) and whether the research suggests directions for growing branches; branches and tasks with their own choice go back with *Reset to default*.
- **Every screen**: in a narrow window the directions open as a sheet with all their actions; the app on a phone shows them read-only, and a tap shows the direction in the tree. When the research is busy or not running, nothing is lost silently: a change says it was not saved and can be tried again. A research older than 1.26.0 keeps the actions in its terminal, with a sentence naming the version that manages directions from the app.
- **Places in Awaiting action**: the small map of a question shows its own points, the location now and the suggestion or the candidates, no longer every place of the family across Europe; points on the same spot stand side by side, so the location now is never hidden under the suggestion. A location conflict says where the location now is and how far it lies from the suggestion (*48 km from the suggestion*). *Don't know* answers a location conflict and an entry suggestion only with research 1.26.0, which takes it there; *None of them* stays with a question of where a place is.
- **Conflicts**: *What the research knows* tells a conflict made by an edit in the app from sources that disagree: *An edit made in the app differs from the research here*, no longer *The sources disagree here* for both.
- **Sections of research following**: each section of the live panel and of the research overview (*At work*, *Awaiting action*, *Latest changes*, *Queue*, and in the overview *Directions* and *What the research knows*) can stay *Expanded*, *Collapsed* or *Hidden*; one choice holds in both places and for all trees on this device. It is set with ⋯ *Customize panel* in the header, by a right click or a long press on a section heading, or in *Settings → Following research*; a click on a heading is a lasting choice too, and *Restore defaults* brings back the usual view, with *Undo*. A new item no longer unfolds a collapsed *Awaiting action*: its heading counts the items and says *2 new*, in amber when a decision or a permission waits. A hidden *Awaiting action* keeps its count as a chip in the panel's header, and a click shows it in the overview until the overview closes. Sections folded in an earlier version stay folded.
- **Sources of a story**: under every story, a person's or a marriage's, one folded section *What the text is based on (N)* holds everything the text rests on: its sources once each (the story's own and those its facts cite, a web address opening in a new window), its facts in words of the app's language (*Death 25 Sep 1857, Oakdale*, never a GEDCOM line) and the author's note. The same in the *Story* window, on the person page (in the saved file as a section that opens without scripts), in the comparison of versions (its changes list the sources and facts added or left out) and read-only in the person's editor; in print and in the family book nothing stands under the text, the story's sources are numbered in the list of sources. A source's bare code in the text reads as its title. The person page also lists such a source among its sources, with *Story* or *Story of the marriage* as what it supports.
- **Surnames**: in a Polish tree a Polish surname pairs the Polish way, *Jaworski* with *Jaworska*, and never gets a Czech *-ová* form (*Nowak*, *Mróz*), now also with the app in Czech; in a Czech tree that has both, *Jaworska* pairs with *Jaworski*.
- **GEDCOM import**: the ids and stamps of a GEDCOM 7 file (*UID*, *CREA*, *EXID*) no longer count as unsupported, and a sentence in the summary says what *Unsupported* counts: the parts of the file Strom does not know and did not carry over, listed by their tag, never the people and partnerships counted above. A couple's status written as *_RELATIONSHIP_STATUS* is read like *_STAT*, also in Polish, German and Czech words, so partners and a separated couple no longer arrive married; a status it does not know stays unsupported.
- **Maiden name**: in the person form of a woman the maiden name field says *Surname at birth*, and the empty married surname says that the surname taken at marriage goes there.
- **Connection to the research**: a big research that needs a few seconds to say its state is no longer reported as *The research doesn't respond*: the app waits up to 15 s for its answer, asks it one thing at a time and keeps the last known state meanwhile; only three asks in a row without an answer say it does not respond. A research that is not running or turns the connection down is said at once, as before. A research from version 1.26.0 on is asked only what changed: the list of what awaits a person, the queue, the off-line work and the other researches are read again only when they changed, so a quiet research sends a few kB instead of over a hundred.

- **GEDCOM import**: the summary says that people of unknown sex were imported, on a line of their own, no longer next to *Skipped records*, where they read as skipped.

## 3.21.0

*Released 3 November 2026.*

- **Polish**: the app speaks Polish: *Settings → Language → Polski*, chosen by itself in a browser set to Polish. Dates, plurals and kinship terms follow Polish, and so do saved pages, the family book and this list of changes.

A large tree is saved as a set of files in folders with an index split by letters, and the save can *Continue in background* with its progress in the toolbar. A line's timeline marks its kinds of events with their symbols (* ⚭ † →); the indexes and the family book put surnames with an unreadable place of a transcription under *Uncertain reading*; a marriage's children on a line page read as one row again, and the *Map of places* shows how many places a cluster holds.
- **Family line in the tree view**: *Lines* in the toolbar chooses one family line, in the same order and names as the tree overview, with a search and *Other families* unfolding the small lines; the person menu offers *Only the … line* (the birth line first). The line's members stand out, spouses who married in stay faint and the others are dimmed in the family view, Descendants and the fan; when none of them is shown, the focus moves to the nearest member with *↩ Back*. A label with × shows the whole tree again, and the line is remembered for the tree. On a phone *More → Family line* opens the list as a sheet.
- **Map**: the kinds of places are four chips, *Births*, *Deaths*, *Marriages* and *Other events* (on a phone one button with a menu), kept per device; the points, the bubbles and *Migration over time* show only the kinds turned on. A town is one point with its house numbers, and the numbers and labels stay legible in the dark and when zoomed out. The person page's map has the same chips for the kinds its places have, in the window and in a saved page.
- **Places**: the dialog groups the places of a town with their houses; *Split into town and house* moves house numbers and streets from the place name into the *House* field, for one place or a whole town after a confirmation; a place without its own location is located by its town.
- **Identifying places**: *Places* shows each place's state from the research, from *Not on the map yet* to confirmed, with *Look up in the research* for one place, a selection or *Look up all not on the map*, and *Why here* with who placed a location and when. A place's spellings, old names, streets and houses form one group under today's name, and *Unify as …* writes them as one place. *Find on the map* tries every form of the name and offers up to five results, a town nearby or a location placed by hand. The research's questions about places are answered in *Awaiting action*, one by one or with *Accept all suggested* and *Keep all*, each with *Undo*. A place the research has confirmed is no longer marked *Check*. After a tree comes from a research, an offer asks to look up the places not on the map. With the research 1.25, *Places* tells the kind of a place's other name: *other spelling*, *old name* with the years it ended, or *other name* still in use, each with its language; another spelling is never shown as a former name.
- **History**: *Settings → Person card* has a *Search* column beside *Show*, with the kinds of history the research searches (regions, places and nine kinds of events) for this tree or as the default; a kind switched off stops at once, a kind switched on waits for the research with its estimate. *Align history with the settings* shows what is hidden, shown again or searched and aligns it in one step with *Undo*; a kind is switched off only after a confirmation that says what it would hide; *Manage history of places* has the same switches under *What the research searches*.
- **Default person and view**: the tree's dialog sets the view the tree opens in, the *Family view* or the *Last used* view on this device, always with the tree's default person; the tree's menu and the tree manager say when a tree opens in its last used view. A reload keeps the tree, the view and the person.
- **Family book**: the *Custom* preset starts from the current settings, keeps its own values and is not overwritten by the other presets; a line under the presets says which one it started from.
- **Smaller changes**: a link from the research's widget opens the tree with the person and the person page, or *Awaiting action* of the tree's research; the research's actions show by their mark whether they open the research's terminal and whether an AI agent starts working there; the footer of the family pages and the person page links to the website; the breadcrumb of the family pages is shorter only on a phone; the fan and the person page's ancestors fit more names into small sectors; further fixes of layout and texts. The family page's marriage excerpt opens enlarged with its scan in the archive below. In the research's queue, a task outside the directions waits until the directions' work is done, and the update offer says that the update does not interrupt a running session or run.

## 3.20.0

*Released 1 November 2026.*

- **Family pages**: a family line as linked pages of the line, its families and people, in the app's window or saved as one file or a set of files (.zip) that opens in a browser without internet. - **Family book**: a line's counts stay on one line, the name forms list the female form once, an approximate founder year reads *about* and an unknown wife leaves no *⚭ ?*.
- **Smaller changes**: a card keeps its height after leaving the fan; further fixes of layout and texts.

Families with too few people for a line of their own are listed as *Other families*.
- **Family line of a person**: the person card and the person page have a *Family line* row with a link to the line in the tree overview and a menu: show the line in the tree, edit it, start a family line from this person, the line's book.
- **Family book**: a new output, a book of chosen family lines, of the whole tree or of a person's ancestors or descendants. The options choose the parts, the detail per person, notes and researcher data, the privacy of living people, the language and the title, show an estimate of the pages and are kept per tree. The book opens in a window with its contents at the side, *Save as HTML*, *Print* and *Change options*, on a phone as well.
- **Person page**: under each register excerpt a link *Scan in the archive* leads to its scan, at the event and in the source card, in the window and in the saved file. On the map close places join into one numbered cluster with one label; the gallery shows a register entry or a document whole on a paper ground, a wide one over two columns; on a wide screen the picture blocks take the whole column beside the contents.
