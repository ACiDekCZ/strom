## 3.14.0

*This version is in preparation; this section grows with every test version.*

- **Read a document**: the *Research* menu has *Read a document…*, and the person menu *Read a document for this person…*. The research agent reads a photo, scan or PDF (several files are pages of one document) and gives a transcription, a translation and an explanation, with the entries framed on the page and illegible spots marked. One reader is faster and cheaper; two independent readers are more reliable and show where they differ. The window can be closed while reading continues, and the result is announced. Reading needs research 1.18.0 or newer (test version 1.18.0-beta.4) with the Claude agent, and runs on the agent's own subscription.
- **Suggest for the tree**: from a reading, each person can be added as new, linked to a person in the tree, or left out; a linked person gains only what is new, and a differing value is kept as another form. Events and relationships can be ticked, and a second reader can read the document before anything is written. Nothing is added without confirmation, and one Undo step takes back the people, the source with the transcription, the crop of the entry and the citations.
- **Read documents**: *Research for this tree…* (in the *Research* menu under *Other actions*) lists the research's read documents, newest first, and each opens its result again. A source written from a reading has *Reading result…* in its detail and in the person's sources, opening the result on the source's own entry. When the research cannot show it (not running, an older version, an archive), a short note says why and the source's own transcription is shown, ready to copy.
- **Phone on the home network**: when the research offers it, the *Research* menu has *Connect phone…* under *Other actions*. It says what appears on the computer, turns the connection on through a window on the computer (or names the terminal command) and shows a QR code for one phone with how long it is valid, and the other network when the computer has two. *Paired phones* lists each phone with when it was last seen, *Unpair* has *Undo*, and a phone paired again offers to unpair its older entry. When the phone has not reached the computer after a minute, a short checklist appears; *Turn connection off* stops it, and the menu and a dot on the *Research* button show while it is on.
- **What will be sent**: besides the people, the list shows the changed sources (with what changed and where they are cited), couples, places with new coordinates and surname variants, each in its own section; a long section shows the first few and expands on request. The count on the *Send* button includes these changes too. An *Undo* pill no longer covers the list's *Close* and *Send* buttons.
- **Clearer menus**: *Actions* (on a phone or tablet *More*) lists what is used most: *Anniversaries*, *Sources*, *Export…*, *For the family ›* (*Family book*, *Poster…*, *Slideshow (TV)*), *Tree: … ›* and, last, *Settings*, now in this one place instead of the toolbar gear. The *Research* menu shows its state in a compact block and at most six actions; the rest opens in place under *Other actions*. *Tree ›* is one list in groups, with splitting, merging and duplicating folded under *Change structure*. *Import…* is in the tree switcher. In the view mode, *Actions* has *Anniversaries*, *For the family ›*, *Tree overview* and *Settings*. *About Strom* is a link at the foot of *Settings*.
- **Source images from the research**: when the research leaves a source's images out of its file, opening the source loads them from the research running on this computer (research 1.18.0, test version 1.18.0-beta.10). They are kept with the tree in this browser, apart from its data, and *Remove from the app* takes them away again. *Sources* has the filter *Image in research* with *Load all from the research…*: counts, size and free space first, then progress with pause and stop. The *Research* menu row *Images in the research · N* opens *Sources* on that filter and shows the progress. The export offers *Source images*: none, only those in the app, or all from the research.
- **Checking the agent's work**: *What was written ›* groups the changes by type, and what the research does not read is marked *app only*. Sources in the research history are links that open the source read-only. A source whose image is not in the app says why beside its load button.
- **Fixes**: switching to *Descendants* right after zooming in with + no longer leaves the tree off-screen. In a tight toolbar the tree name gives way after the search field, so it is no longer cut to its first letter, and a renamed tree shows its new name at once. On a Mac, *Link to a file…* in Export names ⌘S instead of Ctrl+S. At the edge of a research tree, a child with only one parent in the tree shows the stub of the missing father or mother again, on that parent's side. A file sent to relatives without a name reads *A relative sent a family tree*, and the name field is no longer prefilled with a placeholder, not even one remembered by an earlier version.
- **Dates in English** name the month, day first, as GEDCOM writes them: *15 May 1880*, *May 1880*, *about 15 May 1880*, also in the anniversaries of the tree overview (*15 May*). An all-numeric *3/4/1850* read differently in the US and in Britain. Typing a date works as before (*5/15/1880* is still read month first), and the edit fields keep *1880-05-15*.

## 3.13.1

*Released 19 October 2026.*

- **Tree overview**: *Births without a source* and *Line ends* count among the chosen person's ancestors (*3 of 14 ancestors*), the date of death is labelled as counted among the deceased, each birth month column is named with its count (a tap shows it on touch), percentages follow the language of the app, and after a change in the data the keyboard focus returns to the same control.
- **Tree overview, Data quality**: a tree that is not open offers *Validation details*; a finding names the recurring godparent (*Godparent: …*) or the place written several ways (*Place: …*); links in the overview no longer leave a space before a comma; the certainty segments are separated by a thin gap, with new dark-mode shades.
- **Research trees**: in *Ancestors*, a birth graded by the research counts as documented only when the research has proven it, as in the research's own statistics; a note under the table says that the imported family tree is a lead. The births *Where to focus* lists as not documented follow the same rule (*Births not proven: 3 people*), and a birth the research grades offers *Sources* instead of *Cite*. A hypothesis version without a link reads *no link to people in the tree*.
- **Jumping to a section**: *Data quality* from the tree actions or the research overview, and *Ask the family*, bring the heading fully into view under the overview's header.
- **Czech relationship terms**: a sibling with one shared parent is a *polorodý bratr* or *polorodá sestra*, in the tree overview and in *Find relationship…*.
- **Live view**: the directions heading counts the active directions (*Directions · 3 active*), and the person menu calls a running direction *already active*.
- **Research in other trees**: the tree switcher marks every research tree on this computer (*live*, *2 directions*, *3 waiting*, *not running*), and when closed it carries a dot for the other trees. The research panel and *Now* in the research overview name another tree where an agent works or something waits (*Elsewhere*); selecting it switches there. A tree whose research does not answer says *Research not running right now*, what waited last and how it reconnects.
- **Switching to a tree where an agent works**: the tree is followed live at once, with no question; unsent changes of the open tree are sent first, and the tree left becomes editable again. The *Live research* panel names the followed tree in its head and says briefly which tree is followed now and which stays editable. A tree where nobody works stays editable with *Follow live* offered, and its research panel says how edits reach the research: by themselves, with *Send*, or not at all when the research version is only loaded.
- **Directions at work**: when two or more research directions work at once, the *Live research* panel and *Now* in the research overview list each one in a row with its task and since when. Selecting a direction highlights its people in the tree and shows only its *Latest changes* and *Up next*, while *Awaiting action* stays whole and the other directions keep running. A selected direction that stops reads *Not at work now* with the time it was last seen; *All directions* or a second click drops the selection.
- **Fixes**: two quick changes in a row no longer show two Undo notices at once; names on the cards are fitted again once the card fonts are in, so a long name is no longer clipped.

## 3.13.0

*Released 17 October 2026.*

- **Edge of the tree**: a hypothesis whose versions would each link a different family outside the tree shows all of them. The bubble at the edge of the tree and *Above the person* list every family with its versions, people and waiting tasks, each a link to that family; the pill reads *+ 2 families* and offers each one. With Strom Research 1.16 or later.
- **Research for this tree**: one sentence says where the terminal the research opens in is chosen: in the research's settings, with *Settings in the research* beside it. On a computer, with Strom Research 1.16 or later, which brings the choice itself (Terminal or iTerm2 on a Mac).
- **Married surname**: a GEDCOM import keeps the surname after marriage (a second `NAME` with `TYPE married`, or `_MARNM`) apart from the birth surname instead of turning it into another spelling of the name, writes it back on export and to the research, and search finds the person under it too. Cards, the card menu, the person panels and search show the married surname with the birth surname as a quiet note (*née Dvořáková*); *Settings → Person card → Surname display* switches to the birth surname or the married one alone. The family book's index lists the person under both surnames, and *Surname spellings* shows who carries a surname as a married one, beside counts that stay with birth surnames. The person editor has a *Married surname* field under the surname (always there for a woman with a union, otherwise behind *+ married surname*), and under the empty field a suggestion from the husband's surname, in the feminine form where Czech rules apply (*Novák → Nováková*): *Use* fills it in, × hides it and the same suggestion is not offered again. A research version that brings back a married surname emptied here shows it as a row in *Load the research version?*
- **Tree overview**: one overview replaces *Statistics* and *Tree health*. *Where to focus* names what is worth doing next, with a step to fix or show it; *Ancestors* counts known and documented ancestors by generation and follows the four grandparent lines to where they end; *Life and health* (age at death), *Family through time*, *Data quality* and *Highlights* fill in on expanding. It opens from the tree actions, the tree manager, the person count in the focus bar and the research overview, and on a phone as a full-screen sheet. *Ask the family* under *Ancestors* lists the ancestors and their siblings who died in the last 100 years with missing details (date, place or cause of death, place of birth), each with the relation to the chosen person (*father’s father’s brother*), since archives mostly do not publish these records yet; a *Question for relatives* is saved to the person. In research trees, *Data quality* shows how certain the birth, death and marriage facts are according to the research, and the tree menus offer *Data quality* as a shortcut under *Tree overview*.
- **Merging a tree**: married surnames from a file (for example from MyHeritage) fill in the tree, and a married surname that differs on the two sides asks which one to keep, like the birth surname. A person with an unknown given name who stands in the same place in the family as one person in the tree (same sex and surname, dates that agree) is offered as a yellow match in *Review matches* instead of being added a second time.
- **Parent lines**: lines between parents and children no longer cross or run along the lines of another family. Where a line could not be drawn without that, it is left out and the cards link to each other instead: the child's card carries *parents ↗*, each parent's card *child ↗*, and a click brings the other end into view without changing the focus. The poster and the family book name the other end on those cards.
- **Fixes**: the *+ family · N* pill at the edge of the tree counts the family it shows: for a hypothesis whose variants would each connect a different family outside the tree, the number of people and waiting tasks now belongs to the family that appears on a click.

## 3.12.0

*Released 16 October 2026.*

- **Strom archive**: the research mode without an AI agent is called *Strom archive* everywhere in the app, from the choice in *Ancestor research* to its tag in the Research menu. An archive, and a research waiting for its agent, show no agent actions, and their materials and batches are stored as they are.
- **Research overview**: *Now* comes above *Awaiting action*. Work outside the research directions shows in the row *Outside the directions*, and the live panel names the person each run works on; a person the tree in the app lacks is named by the research identifier. Following live ends at once when the research ends, becomes an archive or waits for its agent.
- **Panning from a card**: a drag that starts on a person card moves the canvas, with the mouse and with one finger; a short press stays a click, tap or long-press. The hover card hides while the canvas moves.
- **Hover card**: every union of a person has a line of its own, in the order of the marriages, with its own children.
- **Find relationship…**: the partner of a partner's sibling is a *brother-in-law* or *sister-in-law*, and any other tie through marriages is *related by marriage*, with its path to highlight, instead of "No relationship found".
- **Cards and the person editor**: the † follows the person's own data, not the death of a descendant; the editor lists birth, events and death in the order of the life; the *Symbols* card style, the photo circle beside a two-row name and long places beside the ⇄ pill (another tree has the person) are aligned. The *Anniversaries* count says what it counts ("3 anniversaries in the next 7 days").
- **Fixes**: "Also in:" no longer takes namesake sisters for one person; *Go to stromapp.info* in an exported file opens the app with the tree ready to import; the GEDCOM import summary no longer clips its labels; in Firefox, the notice about edits kept only in the browser no longer waits for the storage prompt.
- **About**: a *What's new ↗* link opens this changelog in the language of the app. At the first start of a new version a *What's new* window shows the points of that version once, with *All changes ↗* and *Don't show next time*; *Settings → Language and appearance → What's new after an update* turns it back on. On a computer with Strom Research, a newer version of the research adds its own points to the window (*Research X.Y.Z*, with *All research changes ↗*), and About shows *Research X.Y.Z • What's new ↗*.

## 3.11.0

- **Linked in the view only**: a family a research hypothesis would link can be shown where the hypothesis puts it, as hatched ghost cards, without changing the tree. Counts, the other views, sending and every output leave the shown links out. An indicator over the view opens the list of shown links.
- **What the research knows** lists a person's hypotheses with their state and versions, and a switch for each version that can be shown.
- **Conflicts from an edit in the app** show two sides, *From the app* and *In the research*; on the computer with the research, either value can be kept in one step.
- **Waiting in the research overview**: chips by kind, two phases (*To do* and *Awaiting a reply*), items grouped by place, and *Sent* written straight to the research, with Undo.
- **Research**: *Last 24 h* takes the research's own count, *This month* includes the scan readers' cost, a refused send says why in the language of the app, and an empty tree offers *Start research with this tree*.
- **About**: in the Czech interface, a link to the users' group on Facebook.
- **Fixes**: a single parent in a marriage chain stands over its own child; deleting a tree right after adding people names the right number of people; bottom sheets on touch screens are opaque from their first frame.
- **Data**: data version 12, as in 3.10; nothing to migrate. The new research parts need Strom Research 1.14.
